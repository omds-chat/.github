## Organization principles

- **Project autonomy:** technical decisions belong to each repository's maintainers; the organization provides the room to discover and collaborate.
- **Authors keep their rights:** rights in code, documentation and design stay with the respective developers or rights holders, and listing does not transfer them to the organization.
- **Listing is not certification:** being listed or hosted only means being findable, not a guarantee of safety, compatibility or continued maintenance; check the source and the risk yourself.
- **Open participation:** taking part does not require membership; permissions follow explicit responsibility rather than enthusiasm.
- **Lightweight governance:** routine collaboration moves directly, while high-impact changes keep room for review, record and rollback.
